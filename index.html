        Change report · v0.1 → v0.3

        Source of truth: SRD v1.0 (Draft). Baseline: kiosk prototype v0.1, backed up unchanged. The gap workbook was used as a checklist and checked against the SRD; see Traceability for where this assessment differs.

        A. Executive summary

        12Screens added (9 on the kiosk, 3 in Client Portal)
        22Screen groups modified
        7Workflows updated or added
        16SRD section gaps closed
        6Sections still partially covered
        1Missing, mismatched or not verifiable
        2Sections waiting on a decision
        17Business decisions open
        • Multilingual support is implemented and tested. English, Spanish, Hindi and French cover the complete visitor journey, with 469 strings each. Automated tests completed walk-in check-in and check-out in Spanish, Hindi and French with no English fallback.
        • All five Missing sections are now represented: §4 device registration, §24 restricted visitors, §44 kiosk status, §46 visitor/admin separation and §48 audit. §46 and §48 remain Partially Covered because real security controls and durable audit storage can't be shown in a prototype.
        • Walk-in order: the §50 mismatch is addressed by following the SRD order by default, with the v0.1 order kept as a configuration option until the Product Owner decides (B1).
        • Other SRD gaps fixed: restricted visitors (§24), server errors (§41), offline home state (§42), staff alerts on badge failure (§29), configurable badge content and per-type templates (§30, §12), photo on confirmation (§26) and "In progress" status (§21).
        • Workflows updated: invited, walk-in, returning and check-out journeys (translation, new outcomes, walk-in order); new device registration; new configuration sync; new kiosk deactivation.

        D. Multilingual implementation

        LanguagesEnglish (default), Spanish, Hindi, French, each with 469 visitor-facing strings. More packs are added as a new dictionary and listed automatically in Client Portal.
        Default languageSet per kiosk in Client Portal; restored after every session (check-in, check-out, cancellation, timeout).
        How visitors change languageLanguage menu in the header next to Help, on every visitor screen including the welcome screen. English is the default; languages are listed by native name. Language is never shown by flag alone.
        Site and kiosk configurationClient Portal › Configuration › Languages: enable/disable, default, order, coverage, preview, missing-key list. Validation prevents a default language that isn't enabled.
        What is translatedInstructions, navigation, buttons, dialogs, field labels and placeholders, visitor types, purposes, form questions and options, validation and error messages, QR and invitation instructions, photo and privacy notes, approval messages, loading, empty, success and timeout states, check-in and check-out screens, badge labels, accessibility labels.
        Not translatedAgreement text (English with notice until approved translations exist); client data such as host names, departments, location and event names; admin registration screens (English by design, DR5).
        Missing translationsFall back to English; counted per language in Client Portal; optionally marked ⚑ on screen for testing.
        Data kept when switchingYes. Field values, answers, selections and errors are stored as values and keys, so a switch re-renders without losing anything or changing the step (test L-04, L-05).
        Layout with longer textChecked automatically: 19 screen states × 4 languages × 2 orientations, with no overflow or clipping found (L-09). Hindi uses Noto Sans Devanagari with taller line height.
        Needs approvalAll Spanish, Hindi and French text is prototype translation and needs native-speaker review. Consent and privacy statements need legal review (LP1, LP2).
